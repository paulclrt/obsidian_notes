## Chatgpt advice
### 5. The crucial transition: stop asking "what should I learn?"

At some point, you need to start asking:
> **"What don't we know?"**
Take a paper you like.
Read it.
Then read the related work.
Then find something that bothers you.
Maybe an assumption seems unrealistic.
Maybe the experiment doesn't test something important.
Maybe their model is inefficient.
Maybe their evaluation doesn't correspond to the real-world constraint.
Maybe a simpler explanation exists.
Then formulate:
**Hypothesis → experiment → measurement → result → interpretation**
